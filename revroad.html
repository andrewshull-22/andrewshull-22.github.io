Venture capital deal sourcing for RevRoad

As a deal sourcing extern, I researched the edtech market to find a startup that fit RevRoad's investment thesis and wrote an investment summary making the case for it.

Role
Deal sourcing extern
Timeline
Jun 2024 to Aug 2024
Sector
Education technology
Deliverable
Investment summary

Overview

Venture firms see far more companies than they can fund, so sourcing is about finding the few that match what the firm invests in and building an evidence-based case for them. My task was to do that for the edtech sector.

The startup I evaluated is not named here to respect the confidentiality of the deal process.

My process

  1. Learn the thesis. I started from RevRoad's investment thesis so every opportunity could be judged against what the firm actually looks for.
  2. Scan the market. I evaluated trends across the edtech market to find areas with real momentum.
  3. Map the competition. I analyzed the competitive landscape to see where startups could stand out and where the space was crowded.
  4. Validate targets. I checked potential companies against the thesis and market findings to narrow the list.
  5. Write the summary. I prepared an investment summary on one sourced startup, covering its viability and growth potential.

How I evaluated the opportunity

An investment summary has to answer a consistent set of questions. These are the areas my analysis covered.

AreaQuestion it answers
Market trendsIs the part of edtech this company serves growing, and why?
Competitive landscapeWho else is solving this problem, and what makes this company different?
Thesis fitDoes the company match the stage and sector RevRoad invests in?
ViabilityIs there evidence the business model can work?
Growth potentialHow large could the company become if it succeeds?

What this shows

  • I can research an unfamiliar market quickly and turn findings into a clear recommendation.
  • I understand how venture firms screen opportunities against an investment thesis.
  • I can write a structured investment case for decision makers.

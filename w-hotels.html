Revenue audit and reconciliation at W Hotels

During my finance and accounting internship at W Hotels by Marriott in London, I audited revenue reports and reconciled daily accounts, catching roughly $25K in billing errors.

Role

Timeline

Location
London, UK
Result

Overview

A luxury hotel processes a constant flow of guest charges, deposits, corporate accounts, travel agent bookings, refunds, and chargebacks. Small errors add up quickly, so the finance team relies on daily reconciliation and regular audits to keep the books accurate.

Working in Oracle ERP, I supported that process across four areas.

What I worked on

  • Daily reconciliation. Reconciled general ledger activity, guest deposits, and accounts receivable for more than 300 transactions.
  • Revenue auditing. Audited more than 150 weekly revenue reports and traced errors back to their source.
  • Billing inquiries. Handled more than 40 travel agent, corporate, and credit card billing inquiries each week, with a 24-hour turnaround.
  • Refunds and purchasing. Maintained more than $15K in monthly refund and chargeback logs, closed more than 50 purchase orders, and managed vendor invoices.

How a reconciliation catches errors

Reconciliation compares what the system recorded against what was actually billed or received. Any difference gets investigated and corrected. The example below shows how a discrepancy surfaces.

AccountLedger amountBilled amountDifferenceStatus

Travel agent commission£310£310£0Matched

Results

Across my audits I identified and resolved approximately $25K in billing discrepancies, helping keep the hotel's revenue and receivables accurate.
